Precision Through Engineering

Mechanical-engineering principles can be incorporated into appropriate fabrication projects to improve accuracy, functionality and structural performance.

  • Accurate measurement and dimensional layout.
  • Load and support considerations.
  • Material and section selection.
  • Structural geometry and alignment.
  • Weld-joint positioning and preparation.
  • Squareness, level and dimensional checks.
  • Practical strength and stability considerations.
  • Functional and aesthetic finishing.
