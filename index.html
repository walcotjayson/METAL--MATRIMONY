Engineering-Based Fabrication

More than simply joining metal — we consider how the finished structure will perform.

Precision Through Engineering

With a mechanical-engineering background, Metal Matrimony applies engineering thinking to fabrication projects wherever appropriate. This helps us move beyond appearance and consider the actual performance of the finished product.

Depending on the project, our approach can include:

  • Accurate dimensional measurement and layout.
  • Load and support considerations.
  • Basic strength and stability calculations.
  • Material and section selection.
  • Weld-joint positioning and practical joint design.
  • Geometrical calculations for frames and structures.
  • Alignment, squareness and level checks.
  • Functional and aesthetic finishing.

Our goal is simple: strong joints, accurate fabrication and designs that look as good as they perform.
