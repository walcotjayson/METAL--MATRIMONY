About Metal Matrimony

Where engineering meets craftsmanship.

Metal Matrimony is a welding, fabrication and custom metalwork business focused on creating strong, functional and aesthetically appealing metal products.

We specialize in custom furniture, shop and office counters, metal frames, gates, railings, repairs, supports and other fabricated structures.

Our approach combines practical welding and fabrication skills with a mechanical engineering mindset, allowing us to consider dimensions, geometry, materials, loads, stability and functionality when developing a project.

Engineering-Based Fabrication

We approach fabrication with measurement, calculation and practical engineering principles.

We Don't Just Weld — We Measure, Calculate & Build.

A good fabricated structure begins with understanding its purpose. Before fabrication, we consider the required dimensions, geometry, materials, expected use and practical construction requirements.

Engineering-based considerations may include:

  • Accurate dimensional measurements and layout.
  • Geometric calculations for frames, trusses and fabricated structures.
  • Consideration of loads and forces acting on fabricated components.
  • Material and section selection according to the intended application.
  • Support-point and stability considerations.
  • Alignment, squareness, level and positioning.
  • Weld-joint positioning and fabrication sequence.
  • Practical material utilization and reduction of unnecessary waste.
  • Design adjustments according to client requirements and available materials.
  • Functional and aesthetic considerations in the finished product.

Our goal is to combine strength, functionality, durability and aesthetics in every suitable project.
