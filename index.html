More Than Just Welding

At Metal Matrimony, fabrication begins with understanding what the structure needs to do. Before fabrication, dimensions, geometry, material selection, loads and practical use can be considered to develop a suitable design.

Our mechanical-engineering background helps us approach fabrication problems systematically rather than relying only on appearance or guesswork.

  • Accurate dimensional measurements and layout.
  • Basic engineering calculations for suitable designs.
  • Consideration of loads and forces acting on fabricated structures.
  • Material and section selection based on the intended application.
  • Geometric calculations for frames, trusses, stairs and other structures.
  • Consideration of stability, support points and practical fabrication.
  • Weld-joint positioning and appropriate fabrication procedures.
  • Attention to alignment, squareness, level and overall finish.
  • Design adjustments based on available materials and client requirements.

For projects requiring formal structural certification or specialist professional approval, the appropriate licensed professional can be consulted.
